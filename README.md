# Interpret, Receive, Resonate, Align

**John Brajer — operating orientation**

A four-stage sequence for processing guidance, signals, advice, and external interpretation without outsourcing judgment.

## 1. Interpret

Determine what was actually communicated.

Separate observation, inference, assumption, framing, and instruction.

## 2. Receive

Allow the information into consideration without requiring immediate agreement or rejection.

## 3. Resonate

Test the information against direct knowledge, evidence, internal coherence, lived context, and relevant goals.

## 4. Align

Choose whether and how to act based on what remains after interpretation and evaluation.

## Purpose

The sequence protects both openness and agency.

It avoids two opposite failures:

- rejecting useful information before processing it,
- accepting external interpretation before understanding whether it fits.
